Many college students silently disengage and drop out due to a combination of academic disinterest, emotional fatigue, mental health struggles, and lack of early guidance.
Faculty often detect these issues only after students have already decided to leave, primarily due to large class sizes and limited monitoring systems. There is a lack of an early predictive framework that integrates behavioural, emotional, and academic data to proactively identify at-risk students.
Hence, this project aims to build a predictive model for student dropout risk, combining empathy-driven insights and machine learning to help colleges detect and support vulnerable students early.
