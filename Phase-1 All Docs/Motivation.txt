 Motivation Behind the Project (PPT Slide Content)

As a student myself, I’ve witnessed many classmates struggle with academic pressure, peer comparison, and the overwhelming pace of engineering coursework.

I’ve seen friends burn out, lose confidence, detach from classes, and even question their future because they felt isolated or “not good enough.”

These experiences made me realize that dropout is not just an academic issue — it’s an emotional, psychological, social, and financial struggle.

I wanted to explore Machine Learning techniques not just as a technical tool, but as a way to identify early signs of student distress and offer meaningful support.

This project allows me to combine my interest in ML with a deeper, personal goal — helping my peers feel seen, understood, and supported before they reach a breaking point.

The ultimate motivation is to build something that can create awareness, encourage early intervention, and contribute to a healthier, more supportive academic environment.
