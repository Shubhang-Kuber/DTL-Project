⭐ Methodology (5-Phase Workflow — PPT Ready)
🔵 Phase 1: Empathy & Problem Understanding

Collect insights from students, parents, and teachers.

Identify emotional, academic, social, and financial factors influencing dropout.

Understand real experiences, pain points, and behavioral patterns.

🟢 Phase 2: Data Collection & Preprocessing

Gather survey responses via Google Forms.

Clean, filter, and convert responses into a structured CSV dataset.

Encode categorical values, handle missing data, and normalize features.

🟣 Phase 3: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Compute correlations between inputs and dropout tendency.

Identify strong predictors (stress, attendance, motivation, etc.).

Visualize trends using heatmaps, boxplots, and distributions.

🟠 Phase 4: ML Model Development & Risk Prediction

Train multiple ML models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost).

Evaluate using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC.

Predict dropout likelihood → Low / Medium / High risk categories.

🟡 Phase 5: Application Prototype for Student Support

Build a simple UI/application that:

Takes student inputs

Runs the model in the backend

Shows risk level

Suggests personalized guidance or support steps

Helps students track academic and emotional well-being.
