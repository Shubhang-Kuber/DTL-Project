⭐ Empathy Map for Student Dropout Prediction Project
🗣 1. SAYS (What the student openly expresses)

“I’m finding it hard to keep up with the subjects.”

“The workload is too much.”

“I don’t feel confident in class.”

“I think I might fall behind compared to others.”

“Sometimes I just feel like quitting.”

“College doesn’t understand what we’re going through.”

💭 2. THINKS (What the student privately thinks but doesn’t say)

“I’m scared of failing and disappointing my family.”

“Everyone else seems to be doing better than me.”

“Maybe engineering was a wrong choice.”

“I don’t feel good enough to continue.”

“If I ask for help, people might judge me.”

“This stress is affecting my health and sleep.”

“What if I drop out? What will happen to my future?”

🚶 3. DOES (Observable actions and behaviors)

Avoids attending classes or arrives late frequently.

Stops interacting in groups; prefers isolation.

Submits assignments late or incomplete.

Reduces participation in activities or events.

Skips mentorship/counseling sessions.

Avoids asking doubts in class.

Spends more time overthinking than studying.

❤️ 4. FEELS (Emotional state and struggles)

Overwhelmed by academic workload.

Stressed due to continuous peer comparison.

Anxious about grades and future career.

Lonely or socially isolated in college.

Guilty for not meeting expectations.

Confused about academic direction.

Fearful of judgment from peers or teachers.

Emotionally drained and mentally exhausted.

⭐ BONUS SECTIONS (Optional but highly impressive in DTL):
🎯 5. PAINS (Core challenges they face)

Lack of confidence

Financial pressure

Poor academic performance

Low attendance

Family expectations

Lack of proper guidance

Fear of failure

🌱 6. GAINS (What they hope for)

Academic improvement

Supportive mentors

Emotional stability

Better understanding of subjects

Motivation and encouragement

A clear sense of direction

Feeling included and understood
